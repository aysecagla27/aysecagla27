I am a Master's student in Computer Engineering with a research focus on **Natural Language Processing (NLP) and Large Language Models (LLMs)**.

My current research interests center on **stylometry, authorship attribution, AI-generated text detection, and human–LLM collaborative writing**. ## Research Direction

My current research direction moves beyond treating AI-generated text detection as a simple human-vs-AI classification problem.

I am interested in investigating the **interaction between human authors and LLMs**, including:

* How human writing style changes when LLMs participate in the writing process
* Which stylometric characteristics are preserved or altered after AI assistance
* How LLM assistance affects authorship attribution
* Whether human–LLM collaborative texts can be reliably detected
* Which linguistic features contribute to AI-assisted writing detection
* How detection methods generalize across different LLMs and writing domains
* How interpretable methods can be used to understand model decisions
